# My Favorite Series: Squid Game

## About the Series
**Squid Game** is a *South Korean survival drama* created by Hwang Dong-hyuk. Hundreds of people in serious debt join a mysterious competition of children's games for a huge cash prize, but losing has deadly consequences. It is ***one of my favorite series***.

### Why I Like It
1. The story is tense and unpredictable
2. It shows how money pressures affect people
3. The game designs are creative and memorable

### Main Characters
